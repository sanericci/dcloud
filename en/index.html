About

Music made for one place.

Dcloud is an instrumental duo — LUMIKO IMAI on composition and voice, sanericci on production and remix. We work from a studio in Kumano, Mie, Japan.

The air moving through a place, the presence of water, the way light shifts. We listen for what exists only there, and turn it into sound that slows your breathing. An inn, a shrine, a temple school, a valley of ripening rice — every place we have written for has had music made for it alone.

Order

Commission
for film, space and brand

Inns, shops, shrines and temples, places of learning, working fields, films. We write music from scratch for that place alone. Not a licence on an existing track, but an original built around the space and the way time moves through it. From a short loop to a long piece that plays all day.

01

We ask about the place, the purpose and the hours it will play. Photographs or footage help.

02

We agree on a direction and send a short demo. This is where we tune the texture.

03

Full production. Length, edits and looping are decided together.

04

Delivery. The scope of use is set out in writing beforehand.
